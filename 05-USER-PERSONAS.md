# ComCreate User Personas

**Document Version:** 1.0
**Created:** January 16, 2026

---

## Executive Summary

Three primary personas represent ComCreate's ideal customer profiles for the $749-$1,499/month website + SEO + LSA marketing service. Each has distinct pain points, decision processes, and messaging that resonates.

---

## Persona 1: Home Services

### "Mike the Master Technician"

**Representative Quote:**
> "I'm great at fixing AC units, but I have no idea how to get my phone to ring more. Every marketing company I've talked to just wants to take my money and give me nothing but excuses."

---

### Demographics

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Age | 47 years old |
| Gender | Male (85% of trade contractors) |
| Education | Trade school + field experience |
| Business Type | HVAC/Plumbing/Electrical contractor |
| Years in Business | 8-15 years |
| Business Size | 3-8 employees |
| Annual Revenue | $750K - $1.5M |
| Service Area | 25-50 mile radius |
| Owner Salary | $80K - $120K |

---

### Psychographics

**Primary Goals:**
1. Consistent lead flow - phone ringing with qualified jobs
2. Growth without chaos - ready to add trucks but needs reliable leads first
3. Reduce dependency on referrals (currently 60-70% of business)
4. Higher-ticket jobs ($8K-$15K replacements vs. $200-$400 service calls)
5. Build business value for eventual exit

**Deepest Fears:**
1. Being scammed by another marketing company (has been burned before)
2. Slow season cash flow anxiety
3. Losing to competitors with bigger marketing budgets
4. Technology overwhelm - does not understand SEO jargon
5. Wasting money during economic uncertainty

**Primary Frustrations:**
- Marketing agencies that "don't understand my business"
- Long contracts with no results
- Not being able to measure what's working
- Leads that are unqualified or outside service area
- Marketing feels like a "black hole" for money

---

### Current Marketing Situation

| Channel | Spend | Results |
|---------|-------|---------|
| Google Business Profile | Free | Gets some calls, not optimized |
| Word-of-mouth | $0 | 60-70% of jobs; inconsistent |
| Basic website | $50-150/mo | Outdated, ranks page 3-5 |
| Angi/HomeAdvisor | $300-800/mo | Hates it; shared leads, low quality |
| Vehicle wraps | One-time | Good local visibility |

**Total Current Spend:** $500-$1,500/month with poor tracking

**What He's Tried and Abandoned:**
- SEO company ($800/mo for 12 months, no ranking improvement)
- Facebook ads (likes but no calls)
- Lead generation companies with terrible ROI

---

### Decision Making

**Decision Timeline:** 2-4 weeks

**Triggers Action:**
- Slow week (looks at empty schedule and panics)
- Lost job to competitor found on Google
- Seasonal shift approaching
- Sees competitor ranking #1 on Google
- Recommendation from trusted peer

**Red Flags That Kill Deals:**
- Long-term contracts (over 6 months)
- No clear deliverables
- Aggressive sales tactics
- Cannot explain in plain language
- No case studies from similar businesses

---

### Objections & Responses

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I've been burned before" | Offer month-to-month, show specific case studies, provide transparency |
| "$500-700/month is a lot" | Frame as cost-per-lead; one HVAC install = $12K revenue |
| "How do I know it works?" | Offer call tracking, reporting dashboard, specific KPIs |
| "SEO takes too long" | Lead with LSA for quick wins; SEO for long-term |
| "Can't I do this myself?" | Show time investment; his time = $100+/hour |

---

### Messaging That Works

**Headlines:**
- "Stop Paying for Leads That Go Nowhere"
- "Get Found First When AC Goes Out at 2 AM"
- "No Contracts. No BS. Just Calls."

**Value Props:**
- "One flat monthly fee, everything included"
- "Cancel anytime"
- "We only work with one contractor per trade per area"
- "See exactly where every call comes from"

---

### Where to Find Mike

**Facebook Groups:**
- "HVAC Business Owners"
- "Plumbing Business Mentors"
- "Service Titan Users"
- Local contractor associations

**Publications:**
- Contracting Business Magazine
- ACHR News
- ServiceTitan podcasts

## Persona 2: Healthcare

### "Dr. Sarah the Practice Builder"

**Representative Quote:**
> "I went to dental school to help patients, not to become a marketing expert. But if I don't figure out how to get more new patients, I can't help anyone."

---

### Demographics

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Age | 42 years old |
| Gender | Female |
| Education | DDS/DMD or Doctor of Chiropractic |
| Business Type | Single-location dental or chiro practice |
| Years in Practice | 6-12 years |
| Business Size | 4-10 employees |
| Annual Revenue | $600K - $1.2M (dental); $300K-$600K (chiro) |
| Owner Compensation | $150K - $300K (dental); $100K-$180K (chiro) |

---

### Psychographics

**Primary Goals:**
1. 30-50 new patients per month
2. Higher-value cases (implants, Invisalign, corrective care)
3. Reduce dependence on insurance (PPO reimbursements declining)
4. Build practice equity for eventual sale
5. Work-life balance (already 45+ hours/week)

**Deepest Fears:**
1. Corporate chains taking market share (Aspen Dental, The Joint)
2. Negative online reviews
3. Falling behind on technology
4. Cash flow during slow periods (August, December)
5. Making wrong marketing investment

**Primary Frustrations:**
- Marketing companies that don't understand healthcare/HIPAA
- No time to manage marketing
- Difficulty measuring patient acquisition source
- Stale, generic website
- Competition from franchise clinics

---

### Current Marketing Situation

| Channel | Spend | Results |
|---------|-------|---------|
| Template website | $100-200/mo | Generic, looks like competitors |
| Google Business Profile | Free | Has reviews, not optimized |
| Social media management | $200-500/mo | Nice posts, unclear ROI |
| Dental/Chiro marketing company | $800-2,000/mo | Overpaying for mediocre results |

**Total Current Spend:** $1,000-$3,000/month

**What She's Tried and Abandoned:**
- Groupon (attracted wrong patients who never returned)
- Direct mail postcards
- Magazine ads
- Radio advertising

---

### Decision Making

**Decision Timeline:** 3-6 weeks (longer due to higher stakes)

**Triggers Action:**
- Slow patient schedule
- New competitor opens nearby
- Current contract ending
- Colleague recommendation

**Red Flags:**
- No healthcare marketing experience
- Cannot explain HIPAA compliance approach
- No dental/chiro case studies
- Pushy sales approach

---

### Objections & Responses

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I already have a marketing company" | Compare results objectively; offer to show gaps |
| "We're different from contractors" | Emphasize healthcare experience, show dental/chiro examples |
| "$500-700 seems low" | Show value vs. $1,500-2,500 dental marketing companies |
| "What about HIPAA?" | Explain compliance approach, testimonial consent process |

---

### Messaging That Works

**Headlines:**
- "Fill Your Schedule With Patients Who Value Quality Care"
- "Stop Losing New Patients to Corporate Chains"
- "Marketing That Understands Healthcare"

**Language to Use:**
- "New patient acquisition" not "leads"
- "Practice growth" not "sales"
- "Patient" not "customer"
- "Professional presence" not "advertising"

---

### Where to Find Dr. Sarah

**Facebook Groups:**
- "Dental Nachos"
- "ChiroSecure"
- State dental/chiro association groups
- "Dental Office Managers"

**Publications:**
- Dental Economics
- Dentistry Today

---

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|

| Gender | Male |
| Education | JD (law) |
| Practice Areas | Personal injury, family law, estate planning, criminal defense |
| Years in Practice | 12-20 years |
| Business Size | Solo to 4 attorneys + support staff |
| Annual Revenue | $400K - $1.5M |
| Owner Compensation | $150K - $400K |

---

### Psychographics

**Primary Goals:**
1. High-quality case inquiries (not tire kickers)
2. Predictable case pipeline (avoid feast-or-famine)
3. Higher-value matters ($5,000+ cases)
4. Professional reputation

**Deepest Fears:**

3. Bar ethics concerns
4. Competition from LegalZoom, Rocket Lawyer
5. Younger attorneys with better digital presence

**Primary Frustrations:**
- Marketing agencies that don't understand bar advertising rules
- High cost of legal PPC ($50-300/click)
- Lead quality issues
- Time drain from marketing meetings
- Pressure to be on social media

---

## Cross-Persona Insights

### Universal Pain Points

1. **Been burned by marketing companies before** - Trust deficit is significant
2. **Cannot measure ROI** - Lack of tracking creates skepticism
3. **Long contracts feel risky** - Month-to-month reduces barrier
4. **Marketing jargon confusion** - Plain language builds trust
5. **Time poverty** - "Done for you" is essential

### Universal Decision Triggers

1. Slow business/empty schedule
2. Competitor visibility online
3. Referral from trusted peer
4. Contract expiration
5. Seasonal timing (new year, busy season approaching)

### Pricing Perception by Segment

| Segment | Perception of $749-$1,499/mo |
|---------|------------------------------|
| Home Services | "About right" - comparable to lead services |
| Healthcare | "Seems low" - used to $1,500-2,500/mo |
| Legal | "Surprisingly affordable" - PPC alone costs more |

### Most Persuasive Proof (All Segments)

1. **Recommendation from trusted peer** (#1 influence)
2. **Specific revenue/call/patient numbers** (quantified results)
3. **Video testimonial from similar business**
4. **Before/after Google rankings**
5. **Money-back or performance guarantee**

## Sales Process Recommendations

### Discovery Questions to Ask

1. "What are you currently doing for marketing, and what's working?"
2. "What have you tried in the past that didn't work?"
3. "How many new [customers/patients/clients] do you get per month now?"
4. "What would success look like for you in 6 months?"
5. "What's holding you back from investing more in marketing?"

### Critical First 30 Days

- Quick wins visibility (LSA setup, Google Business Profile optimization)
- Clear communication on timeline
- Dashboard access for transparency
- Regular check-ins without overwhelming
- Tangible deliverables they can see
