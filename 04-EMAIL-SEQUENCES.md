## Subject Line Testing Notes

**Best Performing Patterns (industry research):**
- Questions outperform statements
- Personalization (name, company) increases opens
- Numbers and specifics build credibility
- "Quick" and "short" reduce friction
- Curiosity gaps drive opens

**Test Matrix for Cold Outreach:**

| Version | Subject Line Pattern | Test Priority |
|---------|---------------------|---------------|
| A | Question about {company} | High |
| B | {Name}, + relevant observation | High |
| C | Specific result + vertical | Medium |
| D | Pain point question | Medium |
| E | "Quick question" variant | Low |

**Avoid:**
- ALL CAPS
- Excessive punctuation!!!
- Salesy words (FREE, ACT NOW, LIMITED TIME)
- Clickbait that doesn't deliver
