Key Insights to Merge

T1 INSIGHT: "Leads" Keywords Are a Goldmine

Terminal 1 discovered that keywords containing "leads" (hvac leads, roofing leads, plumbing leads) have very low CPC ($0.50-$0.90) compared to "marketing" keywords ($6-15). These are high-intent buyers actively looking to purchase leads. This should be prioritized in the initial Google Ads campaigns.

T1 INSIGHT: Add Roofing as Priority Vertical

Terminal 1 ranked Roofing as #2 due to: highest average job value ($8,400), strong "leads" keyword volume (3,200/mo), and 35% close rates. Terminal 2 should add roofing landing page and email sequences.

T2 INSIGHT: LinkedIn/Instagram Automation = Account Bans

Terminal 2's research found that LinkedIn automation tools (Phantombuster, Dripify) carry HIGH RISK of account bans. Instagram cold DM automation violates ToS. Cold SMS is ILLEGAL (TCPA). Email is the only compliant automation channel. T1's strategy should NOT include automated social outreach.

T2 INSIGHT: Use Personas for Ad Copy

Terminal 2 developed detailed buyer personas with pain points and messaging that resonates. T1's ad copy should incorporate:
• "I've been burned by marketing companies before" (address trust)
• "Can't measure what's working" (emphasize tracking)
• "Stop paying HomeAdvisor $50-100 per shared lead" (competitor callout)
