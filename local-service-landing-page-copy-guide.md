## 1. Headline Formulas

### Key Research Findings
- Headlines account for 80% of engagement - "On average, five times as many people read the headline as read the body copy" (David Ogilvy)
- CityCliq increased conversions by 90% simply by changing their headline from vague to specific
- 88% of consumers who conduct a local search on their smartphone visit or call a business within 24 hours

### Master Formula for Local Service Headlines
**[Service Type] + [Location] + [Unique Value] + [Urgency]**

### Headline Types That Convert

#### A. Problem-Focused Headlines
Best for: Emergency services, immediate pain points

**Templates:**
```
"[Problem]? Get [Solution] in [Timeframe]"
"Stop [Pain Point] - [City] [Service] Available Now"
"[City] [Problem]? [X]-Hour Response Guaranteed"
```

**Industry Examples:**
| Industry | Problem-Focused Headline |
|----------|-------------------------|
| HVAC | "AC Stopped Working? Same-Day Repair in [City] - Call Now" |
| Plumbing | "Burst Pipe? [City] Emergency Plumbers Here in 30 Minutes" |
| Electrical | "Flickering Lights? Licensed [City] Electricians Available 24/7" |
| Roofing | "Roof Leaking? [City] Emergency Roof Repair - We Stop Leaks Fast" |

#### B. Benefit-Driven Headlines
Best for: Planned services, installations, ongoing maintenance

**Templates:**
```
"[Benefit] for [City] Homeowners - [Proof Point]"
"Get [Specific Benefit] with [City]'s #1 Rated [Service]"
"[City] [Service]: [Primary Benefit] + [Secondary Benefit]"
```

**Industry Examples:**
| Industry | Benefit-Driven Headline |
|----------|------------------------|
| HVAC | "Stay Cool All Summer - [City]'s Most Trusted AC Installation Since 2005" |
| Plumbing | "Never Worry About Plumbing Again - [City]'s 5-Star Rated Plumbers" |
| Electrical | "Safe, Up-to-Code Electrical Work - [City]'s Licensed Experts" |
| Roofing | "A Roof That Lasts 30+ Years - [City]'s Premier Roofing Contractor" |

#### C. Specific Numbers + Timeframes
Research shows numbers increase credibility and click-through rates

**Templates:**
```
"[X]-Minute Response Time for [City] [Service]"
"[X]+ Years Serving [City] Homeowners"
"Join [X]+ Satisfied [City] Customers"
"[X]% Satisfaction Rate - [City]'s Trusted [Service]"
```

**High-Performing Examples:**
- "30-Minute Response Time for [City] Plumbing Emergencies"
- "15,000+ [City] Homes Cooled Since 1998"
- "100% Satisfaction Guaranteed or Your Money Back"
- "24/7 Service - 365 Days a Year"

#### D. Local/Geographic Headlines
Critical for local search intent - people searching "plumber near me" want LOCAL proof

**Templates:**
```
"[City]'s Most Trusted [Service] Since [Year]"
"Proudly Serving [City], [Nearby City], and [Nearby City]"
"[Neighborhood/Area]'s Go-To [Service] Provider"
"Your [City] Neighbors Trust Us - [X] 5-Star Reviews"
```

**Examples with Geographic Specificity:**
- "Austin's Emergency Plumber - Serving Travis County 24/7"
- "North Phoenix AC Repair - Ahwatukee to Anthem Coverage"
- "Chicago's South Side Trusted Electricians Since 1985"

### Subheadline Best Practices
Subheadlines should expand on the headline with secondary benefits

**Subheadline Templates:**
```
"[Additional benefit] + [Trust signal] + [Low-friction CTA]"
```

**Examples:**
| Headline | Subheadline |
|----------|-------------|
| "24/7 Emergency Plumbing in Denver" | "Licensed. Insured. At your door in 30 minutes or less." |
| "Austin's #1 Rated AC Repair" | "Free diagnostics. Upfront pricing. 100% satisfaction guaranteed." |
| "Same-Day Electrical Service in Phoenix" | "Background-checked electricians. Code-compliant work. Financing available." |

## 2. Value Proposition

### Key Research Findings
- Only 2.2% of companies have effective value propositions
- Home services market projected to reach $1.42 trillion by 2030
- Homeowners making quick decisions need ultra-simple value propositions

### What Differentiators Matter to Homeowners

**Ranked by Importance:**
1. **Speed/Availability** - "Same-day," "24/7," "30-minute response"
2. **Trust/Safety** - Licensed, insured, background-checked
3. **Quality Assurance** - Warranties, guarantees, certifications
4. **Price Transparency** - Upfront pricing, no hidden fees
5. **Local Reputation** - Reviews, years in business, community ties
6. **Convenience** - Online booking, flexible scheduling, clean technicians

### Value Proposition Framework

**Formula:**
```
[Primary Benefit] + [How You Deliver It] + [Proof/Guarantee]
```

### Speed Messaging Templates

**"Fast" Hierarchy (weakest to strongest):**
```
Weak:    "Fast service"
Better:  "Same-day service"
Strong:  "2-hour arrival windows"
Strongest: "At your door in 30 minutes or it's free"
```

**Templates by Urgency Level:**

| Urgency | Template | Example |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Emergency | "[X] Minutes or It's Free" | "There in 30 Minutes or Your Diagnostic is Free" |
| Same-Day | "Same-Day [Service] - Book by [Time], Done Today" | "Same-Day AC Repair - Book by Noon, Fixed by Dinner" |
| Scheduled | "[X]-Hour Arrival Windows - No All-Day Waiting" | "2-Hour Arrival Windows - Your Time Matters" |

### Quality Messaging Templates

**Focus on Outcomes, Not Features:**
```
Instead of: "We use quality parts"
Write: "Parts guaranteed for 5 years - no surprise failures"

Instead of: "Experienced technicians"
Write: "Master technicians with 10+ years average experience"

Instead of: "Great service"
Write: "4.9-star average from 2,500+ verified reviews"
```

**Quality Value Proposition Examples:**
- "Code-Compliant Work Guaranteed - Pass Inspection or We Fix It Free"
- "Factory-Trained Technicians on Every Brand"
- "Lifetime Warranty on Workmanship"

### Price Transparency Messaging

**Templates:**
```
"Upfront Pricing Before We Start - No Surprises, Ever"
"Free Estimates - Know Your Cost Before Saying Yes"
"Price Match Guarantee - Show Us a Lower Quote, We'll Beat It"
"Flat-Rate Pricing - The Price We Quote Is the Price You Pay"
```

**Specific Price Copy Examples:**
- "StraightForward Pricing: Get an exact, to-the-penny price before any work begins. Zero hidden fees. Zero add-on charges."
- "We'll provide three options for every repair - good, better, best - so you control your budget."

### Guarantee Language That Converts

**Research Finding:** Guarantees reduce perceived risk and increase conversions significantly

**Guarantee Framework:**
```
[Specific Promise] + [What Happens If We Fail] + [Time Limit]
```

**High-Converting Guarantee Templates:**

| Guarantee Type | Template | Example |
|---------------|----------|---------|
| Satisfaction | "100% satisfaction or [specific remedy]" | "100% Satisfaction Guaranteed - If you're not happy, we'll make it right or refund your money" |
| Workmanship | "[X]-year guarantee on all [work type]" | "5-Year Workmanship Warranty - If our repair fails, we fix it free" |
| Response Time | "[Service] in [time] or [penalty]" | "At your door in 60 minutes or your service call is free" |
| Price | "Price match + [bonus]" | "Find a lower price within 30 days? We'll refund the difference plus 10%" |
| Clean Home | "We leave your home [condition] or [remedy]" | "Your home will be as clean as we found it - guaranteed, or we'll send a cleaning crew" |

**Real-World Guarantee Examples from Top Performers:**

**The "Fixed Right or Free" Guarantee:**
> "If we diagnose your system inaccurately, your diagnostic is complimentary. If our repair fails within one year, we'll fix it at no charge."

**The "No Frustration" Guarantee:**
> "If the equipment we installed fails to heat or cool your home within the first year, we fix it FREE - parts and labor included. If we don't resolve it within 48 hours, we'll write you a check for $500 for your inconvenience."

**The "Total Comfort" Guarantee:**
> "We guarantee our work for life. If you're ever unsatisfied with our service, we'll refund ALL of your money. No questions. No hassle."

## 3. Social Proof Copy

### Key Research Findings
- Online reviews can raise conversion rates by up to 270% (Northwestern/Spiegel Research)
- 76% of customers regularly read reviews when searching for local businesses
- Testimonials with star ratings perform better than those without
- Center-aligned testimonials above the fold increase conversions by 32%
- Video testimonials can increase conversions by 50-70%

### Review Presentation Best Practices

#### Format Guidelines:
- **Include:** Real name, neighborhood/city, star rating, specific service mentioned
- **Optimal length:** 2-4 sentences - long enough for specificity, short enough to scan
- **Selection criteria:** Choose reviews that mention specific problems solved, not vague praise

#### Review Display Template:
```
[Star Rating - Visual]
"[Specific problem] + [How company solved it] + [Outcome/feeling]"
- [First Name, Last Initial], [Neighborhood/City]
[Service Type Badge: e.g., "AC Repair" or "Emergency Plumbing"]
```

**Example:**
```
*****
"Our AC died on the hottest day of the year. Mike arrived in 25 minutes, diagnosed the problem immediately, and had us cool again within an hour. Fair price, no upselling. This is our new go-to company."
- Jennifer M., North Austin
[Emergency AC Repair]
```

### Testimonial Selection Criteria

**Prioritize testimonials that include:**
1. Specific problem or situation
2. Measurable outcome (time, money saved, etc.)
3. Emotional resolution (relief, peace of mind)
4. Comparison to past experiences or competitors

**Avoid testimonials that:**
- Are generic ("Great service!")
- Don't mention the specific service
- Are too long (5+ sentences)
- Rate below 4 stars

### Testimonial Placement Strategy

| Location | Purpose | Best Testimonial Type |
|----------|---------|----------------------|
| Above the fold (hero) | Immediate credibility | Short, powerful quote with star rating |
| Near form | Reduce conversion anxiety | Review mentioning easy process |
| After service descriptions | Validate specific claims | Service-specific testimonials |
| Near pricing/guarantee | Overcome price objections | Reviews mentioning value/fair pricing |

### Quantity Guidelines:
- **Hero section:** 1 featured testimonial OR aggregate rating (e.g., "4.9 stars from 2,847 reviews")
- **Testimonial section:** 3-6 testimonials (odd numbers often perform better)
- **Throughout page:** Sprinkle 2-3 more near key decision points

### Social Proof Copy Templates

**Aggregate Review Display:**
```
"Rated [X] Stars by [Number]+ [City] Homeowners"
"[Number]+ 5-Star Reviews on Google"
"[City]'s #1 Rated [Service] - [X] Consecutive Years"
```

**Review Platform Integration:**
```
"See what your neighbors are saying:"
[Google Reviews Widget] [Yelp Badge] [BBB Rating]
```

**Testimonial Introduction Copy:**
```
"Don't take our word for it - hear from your [City] neighbors"
"Join [X]+ satisfied homeowners"
"Here's why [City] families trust us with their [service type]"
```

### Case Study Structure (If Applicable)

For complex jobs (HVAC installations, roof replacements, major electrical work):

**Case Study Template:**
```
THE CHALLENGE:
[Homeowner name] in [neighborhood] faced [specific problem]

THE SOLUTION:
Our team [specific actions taken] in [timeframe]

THE RESULT:
- [Quantifiable outcome #1]
- [Quantifiable outcome #2]
- [Emotional outcome]

[Quote from homeowner]
[Before/After photos if available]
```

**Example:**
```
THE CHALLENGE:
The Martinez family in Round Rock had a 20-year-old AC system that was costing them $400+ per month in electricity during summer.

THE SOLUTION:
We installed a high-efficiency Trane system with smart thermostat integration. The entire installation was completed in one day with zero disruption to their family.

THE RESULT:
- 40% reduction in monthly energy costs
- $180/month average savings
- Consistent 72-degree comfort throughout their 2,800 sq ft home

"We should have done this years ago. The installation team was professional, cleaned up everything, and our first electric bill made us wish we hadn't waited so long." - Maria M., Round Rock

[Before: Old unit photo] [After: New installation photo]
```

## 4. CTA Copy

### Key Research Findings
- "Submit" rarely inspires action - it's vague and robotic
- First-person CTAs ("Get MY Quote") can outperform second-person ("Get YOUR Quote")
- Adding social proof next to CTAs boosts effectiveness by up to 20%
- One company increased lead generation by 232% by redesigning their CTA
- Orange buttons are top performers for visibility and energy

### Button Text That Outperforms "Submit"

**CTA Hierarchy (weakest to strongest):**
```
Weak:     "Submit"
Better:   "Get Quote"
Strong:   "Get My Free Quote"
Strongest: "Get My Free Quote in 60 Seconds"
```

### Primary CTA Templates by Service Type

| Industry | Standard CTA | Urgency CTA | Value CTA |
|----------|-------------|-------------|-----------|
| HVAC | "Schedule AC Service" | "Fix My AC Today" | "Get Free AC Diagnosis" |
| Plumbing | "Request a Plumber" | "Stop My Leak Now" | "Get Upfront Pricing" |
| Electrical | "Book Electrician" | "Get Help Today" | "Get Free Safety Check" |
| Roofing | "Schedule Inspection" | "Stop Roof Damage" | "Get Free Roof Report" |

### Action Verb Starters (Most Effective)
- **Get** - "Get My Free Quote"
- **Schedule** - "Schedule My Service"
- **Book** - "Book My Appointment"
- **Claim** - "Claim My Discount"
- **Fix** - "Fix My [Problem] Today"
- **Start** - "Start My Free Estimate"
- **Call** - "Call Now - Talk to a Real Person"

### First-Person CTA Examples
Research shows first-person can increase engagement:

```
"Get My Free Quote" (vs. "Get Your Free Quote")
"Schedule My Service" (vs. "Schedule Service")
"Send Me the Estimate" (vs. "Get Estimate")
"Book My Appointment" (vs. "Book Appointment")
```

### Urgency Copy That Isn't Sleazy

**Principle:** Only use urgency that's REAL and verifiable

**Ethical Urgency Templates:**
```
"Same-Day Appointments Available"
"[X] Service Slots Left This Week"
"Book by [Time] for Same-Day Service"
"Emergency? We're Available Right Now"
"Limited: [Seasonal Offer] Ends [Real Date]"
```

**Examples of Authentic Urgency:**
- "Same-Day Service Available - 3 Appointments Left Today" (only if true)
- "Summer AC Tune-Up Special - Ends August 31st" (real deadline)
- "Emergency Service Available 24/7 - Call Now"
- "Book Your Free Estimate This Week"

**Avoid (Fake/Sleazy Urgency):**
- Countdown timers that reset on refresh
- "Only 2 left!" when there's no real limit
- "Offer expires in 10 minutes!" on an always-available service
- Fake "limited availability" on unlimited services

### Secondary CTAs for Non-Ready Visitors

**Purpose:** Capture leads who aren't ready to buy but show interest

**Secondary CTA Placement:**
- Below primary CTA (clearly de-emphasized visually)
- In exit-intent popups
- At page bottom after all content

**Secondary CTA Templates:**

| Visitor Intent | Secondary CTA | What They Get |
|---------------|---------------|---------------|
| Research phase | "Download Our [Service] Guide" | Educational PDF |
| Price shopping | "Get Our Price Comparison Checklist" | Buying guide |
| Uncertain timeline | "Get Maintenance Tips Newsletter" | Email signup |
| Want more info | "See Our Work Gallery" | Portfolio/photos |
| Trust concerns | "Read Customer Stories" | Testimonials page |

**Examples:**
```
Primary: "Get My Free Quote"
Secondary: "Not ready? Download our AC Buyer's Guide"

Primary: "Schedule Service Today"
Secondary: "Just browsing? Sign up for seasonal maintenance reminders"

Primary: "Book Emergency Repair"
Secondary: "Need to know more? See our service guarantee"
```

### Exit-Intent Popup CTAs
For visitors about to leave without converting:

**Templates:**
```
"Wait! Get 10% Off Your First Service"
[Email field] [Get My Discount]

"Before You Go - Download Our Free [City] Homeowner's [Service] Guide"
[Email field] [Send My Guide]

"Questions? Chat with a Real [City] [Service Type] Expert"
[Start Live Chat]
```

### CTA Microcopy (Text Below Button)

Add reassurance directly below CTAs:

```
[Get My Free Quote]
No obligation. No credit card required. Response within 1 hour.

[Schedule Service]
Flexible scheduling. Easy rescheduling if plans change.

[Book My Appointment]
Your information is secure and never shared.
```

## 5. Form Labels and Microcopy

### Key Research Findings
- Removing one phone number field increased conversions by 79% (Unbounce)
- Expedia increased profits by millions removing one "Company Name" field
- Changing "Request a Quote" to "Request Pricing" increased form clicks by 161.66%
- Proactive microcopy prevents confusion and builds trust

### Field Label Best Practices

**Core Principles:**
- Be specific, not vague ("Full Name" not "Name")
- Use 3 words or fewer when possible
- Match field labels to what users naturally call things
- Add context when asking for sensitive information

### Recommended Fields for Home Service Forms

**Minimum Viable Form (Highest Conversion):**
```
Name
Phone
Service Needed [Dropdown]
[CTA Button]
```

**Standard Form (Balanced):**
```
Full Name
Phone Number
Email Address
Service Needed [Dropdown]
Preferred Date/Time [Optional]
Brief Description [Optional]
[CTA Button]
```

### Field Label Templates with Microcopy

**Name Field:**
```
Label: "Your Name" or "Full Name"
Placeholder: "John Smith"
```

**Phone Field:**
```
Label: "Phone Number"
Helper text: "We'll call to confirm your appointment time"
OR: "Used only to coordinate your service visit"
Placeholder: "(555) 555-5555"
```

**Email Field:**
```
Label: "Email Address"
Helper text: "For your appointment confirmation and receipt"
Placeholder: "you@email.com"
```

**Address Field:**
```
Label: "Service Address"
Helper text: "Where should we send our technician?"
Placeholder: "123 Main St, City, TX 78701"
```

**Service Selection:**
```
Label: "What do you need help with?"
OR: "Type of Service Needed"
Options: [Service-specific dropdown]
```

**Preferred Timing:**
```
Label: "When works best for you?" [Optional]
Options:
- "ASAP - It's urgent"
- "Within the next few days"
- "I'm flexible - just getting a quote"
```

**Problem Description:**
```
Label: "Tell us about the issue (optional)"
Helper text: "The more details, the faster we can help"
Placeholder: "Example: AC is blowing warm air, started yesterday"
Character limit indicator: "0/500 characters"
```

### Helper Text Templates

**For Phone Number:**
```
"We'll text or call to confirm - never for marketing"
"For scheduling questions only"
"Best number to reach you during business hours"
```

**For Email:**
```
"We'll never spam you - just service updates"
"For your confirmation and invoice"
"We send one confirmation email, that's it"
```

**For Address:**
```
"Needed to provide an accurate estimate"
"Our service area includes [City] and surrounding areas"
```

### Error Message Best Practices

**Principle:** Be specific and helpful, not generic

| Field | Bad Error | Good Error |
|-------|-----------|------------|
| Email | "Invalid email" | "Please include an @ in your email address" |
| Phone | "Invalid phone" | "Please enter a 10-digit phone number" |
| Name | "Required field" | "Please enter your name so we know who to ask for" |
| Zip | "Invalid zip code" | "Please enter a 5-digit zip code" |

**Error Message Template:**
```
"Oops! [Specific problem]. [How to fix it]."
```

**Examples:**
- "Oops! That doesn't look like a phone number. Please enter 10 digits."
- "We need your email to send your quote. Please check the format."
- "Please select a service type so we can connect you with the right technician."

### Form Friction Reduction Tactics

**Visual Cues:**
- Show number of fields remaining
- Use progress indicators for multi-step forms
- Gray out optional fields visually

**Trust Signals Near Form:**
```
[Lock icon] "Your information is secure and encrypted"
[Shield icon] "We never sell your information"
[Clock icon] "Takes less than 60 seconds"
```

**Form Header Copy:**
```
"Get Your Free Quote in 60 Seconds"
"Quick question? We respond within 1 hour"
"One form, one call, problem solved"
```

### Optional Field Marking

Clearly label optional fields to give users control:

```
Preferred Time (optional)
How did you hear about us? (optional)
Additional details (optional)
```

## 6. Trust-Building Content

### Key Research Findings
- About page is often the most visited page on business websites
- 80% of attention goes to content above the fold
- Credentials near forms and CTAs have the highest impact
- Contextual trust signals ("Fully Insured: Your Home Protected") outperform generic badges

### About Section Guidelines

**Optimal Length:**
- Landing page about section: 100-200 words
- Dedicated About page: 300-600 words, broken into sections

**Above-the-Fold Must-Haves:**
- Years in business
- Service area
- Key credential (license number, certification)
- Phone number (clickable on mobile)

### About Section Content Framework

**Structure:**
```
1. WHO WE ARE (1-2 sentences)
2. WHAT WE DO (1-2 sentences)
3. WHY CHOOSE US (2-3 bullet points)
4. OUR PROMISE (1 sentence guarantee)
```

**Template:**
```
WHO WE ARE
[Company Name] has served [City/Region] homeowners since [Year].
We're a [family-owned/locally-owned] [service type] company built on
[core value] and [core value].

WHAT WE DO
We provide [service 1], [service 2], and [service 3] for residential
and commercial properties across [service area].

WHY [CITY] HOMEOWNERS CHOOSE US
- [Credential/certification] - ensuring quality and safety
- [Speed/availability benefit] - we respect your time
- [Guarantee] - your satisfaction, guaranteed

OUR PROMISE
We treat every home like it's our own - with respect, honesty, and
workmanship we stand behind.
```

**Example - Plumbing Company:**
```
WHO WE ARE
Anderson Plumbing has served Austin families since 1987. We're a
third-generation, family-owned company built on honest pricing and
work that lasts.

WHAT WE DO
We handle everything from emergency repairs to complete repiping,
water heater installation, and drain cleaning across Travis and
Williamson counties.

WHY AUSTIN HOMEOWNERS CHOOSE US
- Licensed Master Plumbers (#M-38291) on every job
- Same-day service available 7 days a week
- Upfront pricing with no hidden fees - guaranteed

OUR PROMISE
We treat every home like it's our own - showing up on time,
explaining your options clearly, and standing behind every repair.
```

### Credentials to Highlight

**Tier 1 - Always Display Prominently:**
- State license number (with link to verification)
- Insurance/bonding status
- Years in business
- BBB rating/accreditation

**Tier 2 - Display in Credentials Section:**
- Industry certifications (NATE, EPA, etc.)
- Manufacturer authorizations (e.g., "Authorized Trane Dealer")
- Professional association memberships
- Background check verification

**Tier 3 - Display as Supporting Proof:**
- Local awards ("Best of [City]" awards)
- Media features
- Community involvement
- Chamber of Commerce membership

## 7. FAQ Content

### Key Research Findings
- FAQ sections belong near the bottom, after hero, benefits, and social proof
- Accordion format is standard best practice
- Start with 3-5 key questions - quality over quantity
- Place CTAs after key answers and at section end
- Mobile-friendly formatting is critical

### Most Important Questions by Industry

**Universal Questions (All Home Services):**
1. How much does [service] cost?
2. How quickly can you get here?
3. Do you offer free estimates?
4. Are you licensed and insured?
5. What areas do you serve?
6. Do you offer financing?
7. What's your guarantee/warranty?

**HVAC-Specific:**
8. How long will the repair/installation take?
9. Should I repair or replace my AC/furnace?
10. What brands do you work on?
11. Do you offer maintenance plans?

**Plumbing-Specific:**
8. Do you handle emergencies 24/7?
9. Will you need to cut into my walls?
10. How do I prevent this problem in the future?
11. Do you offer camera inspections?

**Electrical-Specific:**
8. Can you upgrade my electrical panel?
9. Is my home up to code?
10. Do you pull permits?
11. How long will the power be off?

**Roofing-Specific:**
8. How long will my new roof last?
9. Do you work with insurance claims?
10. What roofing materials do you recommend?
11. Will you handle the permits?

### FAQ Format Best Practices

**Accordion Style (Recommended):**
```
[+] How much does AC repair cost in [City]?
    [Expanded answer when clicked]

[+] How fast can you get to my home?
    [Expanded answer when clicked]
```

**Answer Length:** 2-4 sentences per answer - enough to be helpful,
short enough to scan

**Pricing Question:**
```
Q: How much does [service] cost?

A: [Service] costs vary based on the specific issue, parts needed,
   and complexity of the job. We provide free, no-obligation estimates
   with upfront pricing before any work begins - you'll know the exact
   cost and approve it first. No surprises, ever.

   [Get Your Free Estimate]
```

**Response Time Question:**
```
Q: How quickly can you get to my home?

A: For emergencies, we typically arrive within [X] minutes. For
   scheduled service, we offer [same-day/next-day] appointments
   with [X]-hour arrival windows so you're not waiting around all day.

   [Schedule Your Appointment]
```

**Licensing Question:**
```
Q: Are you licensed and insured?

A: Absolutely. [Company Name] is fully licensed (License #[XXXXX])
   and carries comprehensive insurance including workers' compensation
   and liability coverage. This protects you and your home. You can
   verify our license at [state board website].
```

```
Q: What areas do you serve?

A: We proudly serve [City] and surrounding communities including
   [City 2], [City 3], and [City 4]. Not sure if we cover your area?
   Give us a call at [phone] and we'll let you know right away.
```

```
Q: What if I'm not satisfied with the work?

A: Your satisfaction is guaranteed. If you're not 100% happy with
   our service, we'll make it right at no additional cost. Our work
   is backed by a [X]-year warranty on parts and labor. We don't
   consider the job done until you're completely satisfied.
```

```
Q: Do you offer financing?

A: Yes! We offer flexible financing options for larger projects
   like [installations/replacements]. Many customers qualify for
   0% interest for [X] months. We also accept all major credit
   cards, checks, and cash. We'll discuss all your payment options
   before any work begins.
```

### FAQ Placement Strategy

**Recommended Page Structure:**
```
1. Hero (Headline + CTA)
2. Trust Bar (Key credentials)
3. Services/Benefits
4. Social Proof/Testimonials
5. How It Works
6. Secondary CTA
7. FAQ Section  <-- Here
8. Final CTA
9. Footer
```

**Number of FAQs to Display:**
- Landing page: 5-7 questions maximum
- Dedicated FAQ page: 15-25 questions, organized by category

```
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got questions? We've got answers. Can't find what you're looking
for? Call us at [phone] - we're happy to help.
```

Or:
```
YOUR [SERVICE] QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

[City] homeowners ask us these questions every day. If you don't
see your question below, just ask - call [phone] or use the chat.
```

## Quick Reference: Copy Cheat Sheet

### Headlines
- Emergency: "[Problem]? [City] [Service] in [Time] - Call Now"
- Scheduled: "[City]'s #1 Rated [Service] - [Key Benefit]"
- Trust: "[X]+ Years Serving [City] | [X]-Star Rating"

### Value Props
- Speed: "At your door in [X] minutes or it's free"
- Quality: "[X]-year warranty on parts and labor"
- Price: "Upfront pricing - the price we quote is the price you pay"

### CTAs
- Primary: "Get My Free Quote" / "Schedule Service Today"
- Urgency: "Fix My [Problem] Now" / "Same-Day Appointments Available"
- Secondary: "Download Our [City] Homeowner's Guide"

### Form Fields
- Phone: "We'll call to confirm your appointment time"
- Email: "For your confirmation only - we never spam"
- Service: "What can we help you with?"

### Trust Signals
- "Licensed & Insured - License #[XXX] | $[X]M Coverage"
- "[X]+ 5-Star Reviews | BBB A+ Rated | Est. [Year]"
- "100% Satisfaction Guaranteed or Your Money Back"

### FAQ Must-Haves
1. How much does it cost?
2. How fast can you get here?
3. Are you licensed/insured?
4. What's your guarantee?
5. What areas do you serve?
