# ComCreate Local Service Business Funnel Strategy

**Document Version:** 1.0
**Created:** January 16, 2026
**Prepared for:** ComCreate.org

## Executive Summary

This funnel strategy document outlines a comprehensive go-to-market approach for ComCreate to acquire local service business clients through a combination of paid advertising, content marketing, and automated outreach. Based on extensive market research, the strategy positions ComCreate as a **value leader** offering agency-quality services at accessible pricing.

### Key Strategic Decisions

| Decision Area | Recommendation |
|---------------|----------------|
| **Primary Offer Price** | $749-$1,199/month (3 tiers) |
| **Contract Length** | 6 months minimum |
| **Primary Acquisition Channel** | Google Ads + LSA |
| **Secondary Channel** | Email outreach via N8N automation |
| **LinkedIn/Instagram** | Manual with template support (not automated) |
| **Target Geography** | West Coast initial, expand nationally |
| **Target Verticals** | HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical, Dental, Legal |

## Part 2: Pricing Strategy

### Research-Validated Pricing

**Market Context:**
- Major competitors (Scorpion, Hibu) charge $1,500-$5,000+/month
- Original proposed pricing ($499-$749) is 40-70% below market
- "Underserved middle market" exists: businesses priced out of $2,500+ agencies but needing more than DIY tools

### Recommended Tier Structure

| Tier | Monthly | 6-Month Total | Includes |
|------|---------|---------------|----------|
| **Starter** | $749 | $4,494 | Website + Basic SEO + LSA Setup |
| **Growth** | $1,099 | $6,594 | + Advanced SEO + Ongoing LSA + Call Tracking |
| **Pro** | $1,499 | $8,994 | + Content Creation + Social + Dedicated Support |

### Value Proposition by Tier

**Starter ($749/month)**
- Professional website ($5,000 value) - delivered in 4 weeks
- On-page SEO optimization
- Google Business Profile optimization
- LSA account setup and initial optimization
- Monthly reporting
- Email support

**Growth ($1,099/month)** - *Recommended*
- Everything in Starter, plus:
- Ongoing LSA management and optimization
- Technical SEO audit and fixes
- Call tracking with recording
- Review management setup
- Bi-weekly reporting calls
- Priority support

**Pro ($1,499/month)**
- Everything in Growth, plus:
- 2 blog posts/month for SEO
- Social media management (2 platforms)
- Dedicated account manager
- Weekly strategy calls
- Custom reporting dashboard

### Pricing Psychology

**Anchor High:** Reference competitor pricing ($2,500-$5,000/month) in sales conversations

**Bundle Value:** Website alone = $5,000-$7,500 value, paid over 6 months

**Risk Reversal:** "Own your website from day one" (unlike competitors who hold websites hostage)

### 4.3 Email Outreach (N8N Automated)

**THIS IS THE PRIMARY OUTREACH CHANNEL**

**Volume Targets:**

- Month 3+: 2,000 outreach emails/month

**Expected Results:**

**Tools Required:**

See **N8N Automation Workflows** document for implementation details.

### 4.4 LinkedIn (Manual with Templates)

**Important:** Direct LinkedIn automation is HIGH RISK for account bans. Use manual approach.

**Daily Activity (30-45 minutes):**
- 20-25 connection requests
- 30-40 messages to connections
- 10-15 comments on relevant posts
- 1-2 posts per week

**N8N Role:**
- Generate daily task list of prospects
- Track engagement in CRM
- Trigger follow-up email sequences

**Target Profile:**
- Title: Owner, President, CEO, Founder
- Industry: Construction, Home Services, Healthcare (Dental)
- Company size: 1-50 employees
- Geography: Target markets

### 4.5 Instagram (Minimal - ManyChat Only)

**Strategy:** Comment-to-DM automation only (compliant)

**Approach:**
1. Run engagement ads to local business owners
2. CTA: "Comment [KEYWORD] for free guide"
3. ManyChat auto-responds with lead magnet
4. Capture email for nurture sequence

**Do NOT:**
- Cold DM business owners
- Use automation tools for follows/likes
- Scrape or automate outreach

## Part 6: Sales Process

### Strategy Call Framework (30 minutes)

**Minutes 0-5: Rapport & Agenda**
- Thank them for booking
- Brief intro about ComCreate
- Set agenda for the call

**Minutes 5-15: Discovery**
- "Tell me about your business"
- "How are you currently getting customers?"
- "What's working? What's not?"
- "What would ideal look like?"
- "What have you tried in the past?"

**Minutes 15-20: Live Audit**
- Share screen
- Review their current website
- Check Google Business Profile
- Look at reviews
- Note specific issues

**Minutes 20-25: Solution Presentation**
- Summarize their challenges
- Explain how ComCreate solves each
- Recommend specific tier
- Show relevant case study

**Minutes 25-30: Close**
- Present pricing
- Ask: "Based on what we discussed, which tier makes most sense?"
- Handle objections
- Book onboarding call or send proposal

### Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Too expensive" | "Compared to [competitor] at $3K+/mo, you're getting the same quality at half the price. And unlike them, you own your website." |
| "Need to think about it" | "Totally understand. What specific questions do you need answered to make a decision?" |
| "Tried marketing before, didn't work" | "What did you try? [Listen] That's actually really common. Here's how we're different..." |
| "Can't afford it right now" | "I hear you. What if we did the Starter tier to begin and upgraded once you see results?" |
| "Need to talk to partner" | "Makes sense. Would it help if I sent a summary they can review? When would be a good time to reconnect?" |
