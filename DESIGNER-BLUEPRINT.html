Create the highest-converting landing page possible for ComCreate's marketing packages. This page is where local service business owners land after clicking our ads. Our goal: convert skeptical, time-poor business owners into qualified leads.

What Research Tells Us

Form Fields

  • 5 or fewer fields = 120% better conversion
  • Each additional field = 20-30% penalty
  • Multi-step forms convert up to 743% better
  • Form + instant scheduling = 66.7% book meetings

Social Proof

  • Video testimonials are 80-86% more effective than text
  • Industry-specific case studies perform 5x better
  • Client logos can increase conversions by 400%
  • 89% of B2B buyers consider reviews essential

Pricing Display

  • 70% of competitors hide pricing - opportunity!
  • Transparent pricing builds trust with skeptical buyers
  • Pre-qualifies leads who can afford services
  • Middle tier should be highlighted as "Most Popular"

Critical Design Rules

DO NOT Include Navigation Menu

"Having a navigation menu provides visitors with too many options" - visitors click away instead of converting. Remove top nav completely from landing page.

DO Show Pricing Transparently

Our audience is time-poor business owners who want to know costs upfront. Don't make them guess or fill out forms just to see prices.

MUST Load Under 3 Seconds

53% abandon pages taking over 3 seconds. Compress all images, use lazy loading below fold, minimize JavaScript.

Reading Level

All copy should be at 5th-7th grade reading level. Pages at this level convert at 11.1% vs 5.3% for college-level copy.

Above the Fold Must Include

  • Clear headline with value proposition
  • Subheadline expanding on benefit
  • Primary CTA button (high contrast)
  • One trust signal (logo bar or stat)
  • Hero visual (results dashboard or video thumbnail)

Purpose: Position ComCreate as the answer to their problems

Elements: Brief overview, 3 key differentiators with icons

Purpose: Reduce uncertainty, show simplicity of getting started

Elements: 3 numbered steps with icons/illustrations

5
Social Proof Section

Purpose: Build trust through real customer proof

Elements: Video testimonials (priority), written testimonials with metrics, case study snippets

CTA: Yes - place CTA after testimonials

6
Pricing & Packages

Purpose: Help qualified buyers self-select, demonstrate transparency

Elements: 3 pricing tiers, feature comparison, "Most Popular" badge on $1,099

CTA: Yes - one CTA per tier

7
FAQ / Objection Handling

Purpose: Address remaining concerns before they become barriers

Elements: 5-7 expandable FAQ items addressing common objections

8
Final CTA Section

Purpose: Last chance conversion with strong value recap

Elements: Benefit recap, primary CTA, guarantee messaging, phone number

9
Minimal Footer

Purpose: Legal requirements, contact info only

Elements: Contact info, privacy policy, terms - keep minimal to avoid distraction

Multi-Step Form Structure

Research shows multi-step forms convert dramatically better (13.9% vs 4.5%). Here's the recommended structure:

Step 1 of 3: About Your Business

What type of business do you have?

[Dropdown: Plumbing, HVAC, Electrical, Roofing, Dental, Legal, Other]

What's your biggest marketing challenge?

[Dropdown: Need more leads, Inconsistent lead flow, Poor online presence, Don't know what works]

Step 2 of 3: Contact Info

Your name

Email address

Phone number (optional)

Step 3 of 3: Book Your Call

[Calendly Embed]

Select a time for your free strategy call

CTA Button Copy Options

Primary CTAs (High Intent)

Get Your Free Marketing Audit Book a Free Strategy Call Get My Growth Plan

Secondary CTAs (Lower Commitment)

See Pricing See How It Works

Supporting Copy (Below Buttons)

  • "No credit card required"
  • "No commitment, no pressure"
  • "15-minute call, no sales pitch"
  • "Cancel anytime, no long contracts"
  • "Takes 2 minutes"
CTA Placement Rule

Repeat the same primary CTA throughout the page: Hero, after social proof, after pricing, final section. One goal, many placements.

Form Design Specifications

Element Specification Notes
Total Fields Maximum 5-6 across all steps Each additional field = 20-30% conversion penalty
Layout Single column Higher completion rates than multi-column
Field Type Preference Dropdowns > Text inputs 15.2% fewer abandonments with dropdowns
Phone Field Mark as optional Increases submission rate
Progress Indicator Show "Step X of 3" Reduces abandonment anxiety
Submit Button Full width, high contrast Use Tech Blue (#1873FA)
Validation Real-time, inline errors Catch errors immediately
Privacy Link Near submit button Builds trust with skeptical users

Mobile-Specific Requirements

Above the Fold (Mobile)

  • Headline: Max 2 lines
  • Subheadline: 1 sentence max
  • Primary CTA: Full width button
  • Click-to-Call: Prominent phone button
  • One trust signal only

Touch Targets

  • Minimum tap target: 44x44 pixels
  • Adequate spacing between targets
  • Buttons in thumb zone (lower third)

Performance

  • Load time: Under 3 seconds
  • Images: WebP format, under 500KB each
  • Lazy load everything below fold
  • Minimize JavaScript

Content Adaptations

  • Stack all content vertically
  • Shorter paragraphs
  • Collapsible FAQ sections
  • Swipeable testimonial carousel
  • Sticky CTA at bottom (optional but effective)
Click-to-Call is Critical

Local service business owners often prefer calling. Make the phone number tappable and prominent on mobile.

Copy Templates & Guidelines

Voice & Tone Guidelines

Words to Use

  • Results, leads, customers, calls, appointments
  • Simple, straightforward, transparent
  • Partner, team, dedicated
  • Proven, data-driven, measurable
  • No-nonsense, no-hassle, no surprises

Words to Avoid

  • Revolutionary, game-changing, cutting-edge
  • Synergy, leverage, optimize (jargon)
  • Best-in-class, world-class (vague superlatives)
  • Trust us (show, don't tell)
  • Simple (when describing their problem - condescending)
Reading Level Requirement

All copy must be at 5th-7th grade reading level. Pages at this level convert at 11.1% vs 5.3% for college-level copy. Use short sentences, common words, and direct language.
