🎯 Hero Section Specifications

⚠️ ABOVE THE FOLD RULE

57% of users spend most time viewing above-the-fold content. You have 3 seconds to capture attention.

Required Elements (Priority Order)

Priority Element Specification
1 Headline 48-72px, font-weight: 700, includes [Location] + [Service] + [Value]
2 Subheadline 18-20px, font-weight: 400, addresses primary pain point
3 Lead Form OR Phone CTA Form: 3-4 fields max. Phone: 24px+ clickable
4 Trust Indicators Star rating + review count + key badges
5 Visual Element Real technician photo or completed work (NO stock photos)

Hero Background Options

Option A: Gradient

background: linear-gradient(90deg, #003cc7, #700f59);

Best for: Clean, professional look. Fast loading.

Option B: Image + Overlay

background: linear-gradient(rgba(7,4,16,0.85), rgba(7,4,16,0.9)), url('hero.webp'); background-size: cover;

Best for: Showing actual work. Image must be <100KB WebP.

Headline Formula

[Service Type] + [Location] + [Unique Value/Urgency] Examples: "24/7 Emergency Plumbing in Austin - 30-Minute Response" "Phoenix's #1 Rated HVAC Repair - Same Day Service" "Licensed Electricians in Dallas - Available Now"

CTA Placement Rule

Include primary CTA at least 3 times on page:

  1. Above the fold (in hero or form)
  2. After testimonials/social proof
  3. Final section before footer

📝 Lead Form Specifications

⚠️ CRITICAL STAT

Reducing from 11 to 4 fields produced a 120% increase in conversions. Every extra field costs you leads.

✅ Designer Checklist

Above the Fold

  • Headline includes location + service + value prop
  • Phone number prominently displayed (click-to-call on mobile)
  • Primary CTA visible without scrolling
  • Trust indicators visible (rating, badges, years)
  • Form OR phone CTA above fold

CTA Design

  • Primary CTA is #3b82f6 (high contrast)
  • Button height: 56px
  • First-person copy ("Get My Quote")
  • CTA appears 3+ times on page
  • Sticky mobile CTA implemented

Form Design

  • 3-4 fields maximum
  • Field height: 48px minimum
  • Font size: 16px minimum (prevents iOS zoom)
  • Trust elements near form
  • Clear submit button with action text

Mobile Requirements

  • Touch targets: 48x48px minimum
  • Single column layout
  • No horizontal scrolling
  • Sticky bottom CTA bar
  • Click-to-call works

Social Proof

  • Star rating with review count above fold
  • 3+ testimonials with real names
  • Trust badges near form
  • Google/BBB badges visible

Performance

  • Total page weight: <1MB
  • Images: WebP format, <100KB each
  • Hero image has width/height set (prevents CLS)
  • No render-blocking resources above fold
